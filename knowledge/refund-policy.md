## Refund Policy

Customers may return an item within **30 days** of purchase, provided they present a **receipt**. The returned items must be **unused**. Refunds will be processed and land in the customer's account within **5 working days** after the return is received.

**Faulty goods** are exempt from the 30‑day window and may be returned according to the faulty‑goods procedure.
